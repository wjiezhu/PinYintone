Privacy Policy — Pinyintone

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Pinyintone is a Mandarin pronunciation training app developed as part of academic research at Shanghai International Studies University. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

1. What we collect

DataPurposeLinked to you?
Practice results: pitch (F0) value sequences, pronunciation scores, target words, timestamps To give you feedback in the app and to study how learners acquire Mandarin tones Pseudonymous — tied to a random device identifier
A random device identifier, and your Sign in with Apple user identifier To keep your progress consistent across sessions and devices Yes (pseudonymous identifier only)
Display name (optional) and preferred language To personalise the interface Yes, if you provide it

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We never upload your audio recordings. Your voice is processed entirely on your device; only numerical pitch values and scores leave the phone.
  • We do not collect your email address, contacts, photos, location, or browsing activity.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Microphone

The app requests microphone access solely to analyse your pronunciation in real time. Audio is analysed on-device and is not stored or transmitted.

4. Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is used to create your account. We receive only an anonymous Apple user identifier, and your name if you choose to share it. We never receive your Apple ID password.

5. Where data is stored

Practice data is stored on servers operated by Render and Neon (PostgreSQL), located in the European Union. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Research use

Aggregated, pseudonymous practice data may be analysed and reported in an academic thesis and related publications. No individual participant is identifiable in any published result. Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every practice record from our servers and from your device. It cannot be undone.

You may also email the request to the address below and we will delete your data within 30 days.

8. Children

Pinyintone is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the date above.
