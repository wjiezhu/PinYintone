Privacy Policy — Pinyintone

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pinyintone is a Mandarin pronunciation training app developed as part of academic research at Shanghai International Studies University. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

1. What we collect

DataPurposeLinked to you?
Practice results: pitch (F0) value sequences, pronunciation scores, target words, timestamps To give you feedback in the app and to study how learners acquire Mandarin tones Pseudonymous — tied to a random device identifier
A random device identifier, and your Sign in with Apple user identifier To keep your progress consistent across sessions and devices Yes (pseudonymous identifier only)
Display name (optional) and preferred language To personalise the interface Yes, if you provide it

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not upload recordings from your everyday practice. Your voice is processed on your device; only numerical results such as scores leave the phone. The only exception is described in section 6: recordings made during an in-person research session, and only if you have explicitly agreed to it in the study consent form.
  • We do not collect your email address, contacts, photos, location, or browsing activity.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Microphone

The app requests microphone access solely to analyse your pronunciation in real time. Audio is analysed on-device. It is not stored or transmitted, except for in-person research session recordings that a study participant has explicitly agreed to upload (see section 6).

4. Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is used to create your account. We receive only an anonymous Apple user identifier, and your name if you choose to share it. We never receive your Apple ID password.

5. Where data is stored

Practice data is stored on servers operated by Render (application server, Oregon, United States) and Neon (PostgreSQL database, AWS us-east-1, United States). Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Research use

Aggregated, pseudonymous practice data may be analysed and reported in an academic thesis and related publications. No individual participant is identifiable in any published result.

Taking part in the research study is a separate, optional step inside the app with its own consent form. Study participants may do an in-person session (a short reading task, practice, and the same reading task again). Only if the consent form you agreed to says so, the recordings from that session are uploaded so that the researcher can check scores by ear. These recordings are kept only on the research server, used only by the research team, never published, and deleted when you leave the study. Recordings from everyday practice are never uploaded.

You can leave the study at any time in Settings → Study participation, or delete your account (section 7).

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and every practice record from our servers and from your device. It cannot be undone.

You may also email the request to the address below and we will delete your data within 30 days.

8. Children

Pinyintone is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the date above.
